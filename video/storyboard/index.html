What the Claude films do

Ten films from Anthropic's channel, Feb to Sep 2026, studied frame by frame and by their sound (19 Nov). Rules we take, in our own look.

  • An app step is the real element, not a text card, cropped to 40–60 % of the frame width on a plain ground, with the user's sentence beside it.

    Introducing Claude Opus 4.6; Claude Fable 5 and 5.1

  • Every request is paired with its result. No prompt is shown without what it produced.

    Opus 4.6 shows 13 outputs, each with its request

  • How it works is drawn with named real things, joined by wires, and a highlight travels to the one in use. No outlined boxes in any of the ten films.

    Model Hardware Standard

  • A case opens on the thing itself, filling the frame, and is named within about a second; the words name something visible.

    Opus 4.6

  • One highlighter marks the thing that matters (yellow, about #F6E755), or the rest dims. Never bold.

    Translating Claude's thoughts; When AIs act emotional

  • Pictures of real objects, never an icon set or emoji.

    Translating Claude's thoughts

  • Three type voices: a book serif for story lines, a plain sans for the app, monospace only for machine things such as times. Text cuts on or fades in 2–3 frames; no slide, no bounce.

    All ten

  • Hard cuts inside a section (92–100 %). One shrinking montage at most, to open or to land on the end card.

    The live-action films

  • Music marks the structure: it changes on section cuts; no whoosh on text or UI in any of the ten. Loudness about −14 LUFS.

    Measured on all ten

  • The end card is quiet: the mark, then the name, held 2–3 s; the music resolves on the cut; no drum hit.

    All ten
