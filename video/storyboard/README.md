# Video 1 storyboard

The shot-by-shot plan that the team approves before a cut is rendered. `index.html` is the source of the page published at [Video 1 storyboard](https://claude.ai/artifact/S51wmbPejvLMuXodXr4myL), where each shot is marked Approve or Change. `img/` holds one real frame per shot: licensed stock, or the reference for a shot we film.

- **v5 (18 Nov):** steady real moments, then a text-only burst of questions, then the wallet story on its own footage. It answers the team's rejection of v4: a fast footage montage, one clip reused, and drawn app screens.
- **v5.1 (18 Nov):** revised from the research in [../research/](../research/):
  - cuts on the bar;
  - readable questions, which stack instead of swapping;
  - the real app, pushed in until it fills the frame;
  - alerts that carry their picture;
  - real sounds only;
  - three proof cases;
  - every box fitted by hand to its object;
  - the end card with the official logos.
