What changes from v4

“I don't want the constant quick flick of examples.”

The opening is four steady real moments, 2 s each (one bar of the music), like the start you liked in v3. No footage flicks.

“The flick fast is the text only… more examples of the text.”

The fast part is text only: the questions arrive faster and faster with the music, then a hard stop and silence. Two ways to show them; play both in act 2.

“The first 30 seconds you just reuse the video.”

No clip is used twice. Each beat of the wallet story has its own footage: the table, the photos, the sofa, the door, the street.

“Taking a picture, you literally just screenshot the video.”

We film the real actions on Thursday: a hand photographing the wallet, someone asking the phone, someone leaving. The app parts are screen recordings of the real prototype, zoomed in so they can be read.

What the research changed (v5.1)

Rules from people who make launch films, home-camera brands' own videos and the craft skills we have installed. Shots they changed carry a “revised after research” tag and a “Why” line.

  • Cut on the bar. At 120 BPM a bar is 2 s; footage cuts land on downbeats, never crossfade between scenes.

    Beat-editing guides; HyperFrames motion reference

  • Text you can read. About 17 characters a second in Portuguese; a 0,25 s card can't be read. Titles hold long enough to read twice, then stay still.

    Netflix Portuguese timed-text guide

  • No flashing. At most three full-frame changes a second, and no white flashes; the hard stop is silence, not white.

    W3C WCAG 2.3.1

  • Real app, readable. Record the real prototype, push in until it fills the frame, and start the reading time only when the camera stops.

    HyperFrames launch reference; product-demo zoom guides

  • The sentence and the crop are the hero. Alerts read “who or what, where” and carry their picture; a wide view plus a close-up inset.

    Google Nest, Ring, Arlo, Wyze

  • Real sounds, only on changes. Shutter, door, tap, vibration; no beeps or chimes; one riser in the whole film; mix at −16 to −14 LUFS.

    HyperFrames sound reference; Google Pixel sound design

  • Three proof cases, 8 s each. Safety, care and memory; the dog moves out. Older people framed around independence.

    Launch-film structure; Kami's ageing-in-place framing

  • No claims we can't show. “Ninguém configura nada” becomes “A app propõe o que vigiar”; the privacy line waits for the team's yes.

    AGENTS.md; brands state privacy once, plainly

Thursday

What we film, shot by shot

Seven short takes, about an hour with one phone and one helper. Each has a stock fallback, so the film never waits on a take that fails.

For every take

  • Phone in landscape, 4K at 30 fps, lens wiped clean.
  • Lock exposure and focus before pressing record (press and hold on the subject).
  • Daylight plus every room light on; nothing dark, nothing back-lit.
  • Hold still 2 s before and 2 s after each action. Three takes of each.
  • Only hands, backs and shoulders. Anyone whose face shows has agreed, or we blur them.
  • No brand logos in frame; turn labels away.

Anything else

Music, pace, length, a shot you want added. Saved as you type.
