What changes from v5.1

“The pace and the effects are slow.”

Shot lengths now come from six product films measured frame by frame (Pixel, Apple, Anthropic, Ring, Nest, Linear): an action the viewer must follow 1,5–2 s, one app screen 1–1,5 s, almost every cut hard, text in 6–8 frames. The film is about 44 s.

“The opening doesn't hook.”

One real moment, someone about to leave home, with the questions popping fast over it, one per beat and then two, then a hard stop. The speed is in the text, as you asked in v4, not in a flicker of clips.

“Text pop-ups have no sound; the keys and the dog have no Foley.”

Every visual event has its sound: a click on every text pop, keys, the door, taps on glass, the phone's buzz, a tick per view counted, a logo sting on the end card.

“The wallet section looks fake.”

No stills and no wallet. Real stock phones with green screens, the real app tracked onto them with the hand and the notch on top, and the keys as the story. Shot 04 is already built (proof A).

What the research and the measurements say

From video/research/compositing-playbook.md and pacing.md (19 Nov).

  • Speed comes from the opening and short app screens, not from cutting the actions the viewer must understand. No measured film speeds up towards its end.

    Six films measured shot by shot

  • Hard cuts, 90–100 %. Soft transitions only where time passes and at the end.

    Pixel, Ring, Anthropic, Apple, Nest

  • Text in 6–8 frames. Words in a quick series hold under a second; a line on its own holds about 2 s.

    Pixel's “Little / Big / Huge mistakes”; Nest; Anthropic

  • A sound for every visual event, landing on the frame of contact, never before it; whooshes peak where the move is fastest; the mix at −14 LUFS.

    ITU-R BT.1359; EBU R128; sound-design guides

  • Phones in ads are filmed blank and the screen is replaced. Ours are stock green-screen phones, tracked (2 px accuracy in 4K tests) with the thumb and notch kept on top.

    Frame.io, ProVideo Coalition, Mocha guides

  • The real product, never a mock-up. Every screen is a recording of the prototype; things the prototype can't do yet are labelled “conceito”.

    AGENTS.md
